# Billing and Payment

## Accepted Payment Methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. We also accept Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, and store credit. Cryptocurrency payments are not accepted at this time.

For orders over $1,000, we offer financing through Affirm. The exact APR and term shown at checkout depends on your credit profile and is determined by Affirm — we do not have visibility into individual financing decisions.

## When You Are Charged

For in-stock items, your payment method is charged when the order ships. Pre-orders and back-orders place a temporary authorization hold at checkout; the actual charge does not occur until fulfillment. Holds typically release within 5 business days if the order is canceled.

## Sales Tax

We collect sales tax in every U.S. state where required by law. The exact tax rate is calculated at checkout based on your shipping address. We do not collect VAT on international orders — the recipient is responsible for any duties or import taxes at delivery.

## Invoices

A receipt is emailed to you at the time of purchase. You can also download a PDF invoice for any past order from the order details page. Invoices include our business address and tax registration number for accounting purposes.

## Disputes and Chargebacks

If you see an unfamiliar charge, please contact support before initiating a chargeback. We can often resolve the issue (for example, recognizing a family member's order or correcting a duplicate charge) within 1 business day. Chargebacks filed without contacting us first may delay refunds because they trigger a separate bank investigation.

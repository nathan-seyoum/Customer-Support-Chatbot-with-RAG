# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Eligibility

We accept returns within **30 days** of the original delivery date. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories that shipped with the product. Final-sale and clearance items are not eligible for return.

Personalized or custom-made items cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged or defective.

## How to Start a Return

1. Sign in to your account and open the order from your order history.
2. Click **Start a Return** next to the item you want to send back.
3. Choose a reason for the return and whether you want a refund or a replacement.
4. Print the prepaid return label that we email you.

You can also initiate a return without an account by entering your order number and the email used at checkout on the **Returns** page.

## Refund Timing

Refunds are issued to your original payment method **within 5–7 business days** after we receive and inspect the returned item. Credit-card refunds may take an additional 1–2 billing cycles to appear on your statement, depending on your bank.

If you paid with store credit, the refund is added back to your store-credit balance immediately upon inspection.

## Return Shipping

Domestic returns are free — we include a prepaid USPS label in your return confirmation email. International return shipping is the customer's responsibility unless the return is due to a defect or our shipping error.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrived damaged or defective, contact support within **7 days** of delivery. We will arrange a free replacement or full refund, including any original shipping costs, and you will not be required to return the damaged item in most cases.

# Contacting Support

## Channels

| Channel    | Hours                             | Typical Response Time   |
|------------|-----------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Live Chat  | Mon–Fri 6 AM – 6 PM Pacific       | < 2 minutes             |
| Email      | 24/7 intake                       | Within 1 business day   |
| Phone      | Mon–Fri 7 AM – 5 PM Pacific       | < 5 minutes typical hold|

Live chat is available from any page on the website using the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner. The email address for support is `help@example.com`. The support phone number is **1-800-555-0142**.

We do not provide support via direct message on social media. Mentions and DMs to our brand accounts are not monitored for support tickets.

## What to Include

When you contact us, please include:

- Your **order number** (if applicable), formatted like `ORD-XXXXXX`.
- The email address used on the order.
- A clear description of the issue, including any error messages.
- Photographs, if the issue is about damage or a defective item.

This information lets us resolve most issues in a single response without back-and-forth.

## Escalations

If your issue has not been resolved after two responses from a support representative, you can ask to escalate. Escalations are reviewed by a senior support specialist within one business day. We do not have a separate "supervisor" hotline — escalations happen within the same ticket so all history is preserved.

## After-Hours Emergencies

For order-related emergencies outside business hours (for example, a wrong item shipped to a wedding the next day), email `urgent@example.com` with **URGENT** in the subject line. An on-call representative will respond within 2 hours, 7 days a week.

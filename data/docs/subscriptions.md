# Subscriptions

## How Subscriptions Work

A subscription delivers selected products on a recurring schedule (monthly, every two months, or quarterly). You can subscribe from the product page by choosing **Subscribe & Save** and selecting your preferred interval.

Subscription orders receive an automatic **10% discount** on every recurring shipment. The first order is charged immediately; subsequent orders are charged on the renewal date.

## Managing a Subscription

You can pause, skip, change the interval, swap products, or cancel a subscription at any time from **Account Settings → Subscriptions**. Changes must be saved **at least 24 hours before the next renewal date** to apply to that upcoming order.

There is no penalty for canceling a subscription and no minimum number of shipments required.

## Failed Payments

If a subscription renewal payment fails (for example, an expired card), we retry the charge once after 24 hours and again after 72 hours. After three failed attempts the subscription is paused and you are notified by email. The subscription resumes automatically once you update the payment method.

## Subscription Refunds

If a subscription order ships before you cancel it, the order is treated like any other order under our 30-day return policy. We do not retroactively refund prior subscription shipments simply because you canceled the subscription afterward.

# Accounts, Login, and Password

## Creating an Account

You can create an account at any time from the **Sign In** page. We require a valid email address and a password of at least **10 characters** with at least one number and one symbol. A confirmation email is sent immediately; the confirmation link expires after 24 hours.

You can also sign in with Google or Apple. Linking a social provider does not change your existing order history — orders placed as a guest with the same email are associated with the account automatically.

## Forgot Password

Use the **Forgot Password** link on the sign-in page. We will email you a one-time reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. If you do not receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder and verify the address on file is correct.

## Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is optional but strongly recommended. We support time-based one-time passwords (TOTP) using apps like Google Authenticator, 1Password, and Authy. SMS-based 2FA is not supported.

You can enable 2FA from **Account Settings → Security**. If you lose access to your authenticator app, contact support — we will verify your identity using government ID and the last four digits of the payment method on your most recent order before disabling 2FA.

## Email Changes

To change the email on your account, go to **Account Settings → Profile**. We will send a verification link to the new address; the change does not take effect until you click that link. The old email remains the address of record until verification is complete.

## Deleting an Account

You can delete your account from **Account Settings → Privacy → Delete Account**. Deletion is final after a 14-day grace period during which you can cancel the request by signing in. We retain transaction records as required by law (typically 7 years for tax purposes) but anonymize them so they are no longer linked to your personal profile.

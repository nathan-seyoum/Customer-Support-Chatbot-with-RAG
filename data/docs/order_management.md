# Managing Your Order

## Order Statuses

| Status            | Meaning                                                          |
|-------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Pending           | Order received; payment authorization in progress.               |
| Confirmed         | Payment authorized; not yet handed to fulfillment.               |
| In Fulfillment    | Warehouse is picking and packing the order.                      |
| Shipped           | Carrier has the package; tracking is active.                     |
| Delivered         | Carrier has reported successful delivery.                        |
| Canceled          | Order canceled before shipment.                                  |
| Refunded          | Refund issued in full or in part.                                |

## Canceling an Order

You can cancel an order yourself if it is in **Pending** or **Confirmed** status. Once an order moves to **In Fulfillment**, cancellation is no longer available — you'll need to wait for delivery and then start a return.

To cancel, open the order from your order history and click **Cancel Order**. The authorization hold on your payment method is released automatically; it can take up to 5 business days for your bank to remove the hold.

## Modifying an Order

You can edit the shipping address and add a gift message before the order reaches **In Fulfillment**. You cannot change items, quantities, or payment method once the order is placed — instead, cancel and re-order.

## Combining Orders

We are unable to combine separate orders into a single shipment, even if they were placed within a few minutes of each other. This is a warehouse-system limitation: each order is treated as an independent fulfillment job. If you want a single shipment, please cancel both orders and place a new combined one.

## Splitting Shipments

Large orders are sometimes split across multiple shipments to reduce delivery time. You are not charged additional shipping when this happens. Each shipment has its own tracking number and you will receive a separate shipping confirmation email per package.

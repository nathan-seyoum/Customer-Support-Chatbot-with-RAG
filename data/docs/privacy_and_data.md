# Privacy and Data

## What We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

- **Account data**: name, email, password hash, phone number (optional), and saved addresses.
- **Order data**: items purchased, prices paid, shipping address, and order timestamps.
- **Payment data**: last four digits and expiration of the card and a tokenized reference. **We do not store full credit card numbers** — those are held by our PCI-DSS-compliant payment processor.
- **Browsing data**: pages viewed, items added to cart, and search queries, used to improve the site experience.
- **Communications**: emails, chats, and call recordings (where consent is given) with our support team.

## How We Use It

We use your data to fulfill orders, provide support, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and — with your consent — to send marketing communications. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

## Data Sharing

We share limited data with the following service providers, only as needed to operate the business:

- Payment processors (e.g. Stripe) for payment authorization.
- Shipping carriers (USPS, UPS, DHL) to deliver orders.
- Email infrastructure providers to send transactional and marketing email.
- Analytics providers, in an aggregated or pseudonymized form.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Access the data we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate data.
- Delete your data (subject to legal retention requirements; see the Accounts policy).
- Opt out of targeted advertising and "sale" of personal information (we already do not sell, but you can confirm your status).
- Port your data to another service.

To exercise any of these rights, email `privacy@example.com` from the address on file or use the **Privacy Request** form in your account settings. We respond within 30 days.

## Data Retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. Transactional records are retained for a minimum of 7 years for tax and audit purposes after account deletion, in anonymized form. Support communications are retained for 3 years.

# Product Warranty

## Standard Warranty

Every product we sell comes with a **1-year limited warranty** against manufacturing defects, starting from the date of delivery. The warranty covers parts and labor for repair or replacement at our discretion, and does not cover damage from accident, misuse, normal wear, or unauthorized modification.

## Extended Warranty

An optional **3-year extended protection plan** is available at checkout for most items. Extended plans cover accidental damage (drops, spills) in addition to manufacturing defects, with a small per-incident deductible of $25.

Extended warranties are administered by our partner, Assurant. Claims are filed directly with Assurant using the policy number emailed to you at the time of purchase.

## Filing a Warranty Claim

1. Locate your order number and the serial number of the product (printed on the device or the original packaging).
2. Visit the **Warranty Claim** page and fill out the form, including a description of the issue and photographs if applicable.
3. We will respond within 2 business days with next steps, which may include a shipping label to return the item for inspection.

## What's Not Covered

- Damage caused by liquid contact (unless covered under an extended plan).
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect function (scratches, dents, etc.).
- Damage from unauthorized repair attempts.
- Products without a verifiable proof of purchase from us or an authorized reseller.

## Replacement vs. Repair

We repair items when a manufacturer-authorized repair is available within a reasonable timeframe; otherwise, we replace the item with the same model or, if unavailable, a model of equal or greater value. Replacements do not extend the original warranty period.

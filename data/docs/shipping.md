# Shipping Information

## Domestic Shipping (United States)

| Method               | Estimated Delivery | Cost                            |
|----------------------|--------------------|---------------------------------|
| Standard Ground      | 3–5 business days  | Free on orders over $50         |
| Expedited 2-Day      | 2 business days    | $9.99 flat rate                 |
| Overnight            | Next business day  | $19.99 flat rate                |

Orders placed before **2:00 PM Pacific Time** on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after that cut-off or on weekends ship the next business day.

## International Shipping

We ship to over 40 countries via DHL and UPS. Standard international delivery takes **7–14 business days**. Customs, duties, and import taxes are the recipient's responsibility and are not included in our shipping fees.

A small list of products cannot be shipped internationally due to manufacturer or export restrictions; these are clearly labeled on the product page.

## Tracking

Once your order ships you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can also view tracking information on the order details page in your account.

If your tracking has not updated for more than 5 business days, contact us — we will open a trace with the carrier and either reship the order or refund you.

## Address Changes

We can change a shipping address only if the order has not yet entered the **"In Fulfillment"** status. Sign in to your account and edit the order, or contact support immediately. Once an order is in fulfillment we are unable to redirect it.

## Lost or Stolen Packages

If a package is marked delivered but you cannot find it, please wait 48 hours (carriers occasionally mark packages delivered prematurely) and check with neighbors and your building's mail area. If it still has not turned up, file a report from your order details page and we will work with the carrier on a claim.
